# Compliance case workflow

Cases represent renewal, initial licensing, amendment, bond, annual report,
financial document, deficiency, information response, surrender, and other
work. They stay open across email threads and are linked to an obligation and
legal entity.

The explicit stage graph follows the operational process from due identification
through vendor questions, internal answers, document checklist, packet, form,
signature, recorded external submission, vendor/regulator review, renewed
evidence, inventory update, and completion.

Evidence gates prevent stage skipping. Signature-required forms must have signed
evidence before submission stages. Submitted status requires manually recorded
external evidence. Vendor receipt is not regulator approval. Completion requires
renewed evidence or an approved close reason. Every transition records actor,
reason, evidence, and duration in the prior stage.

Milestone 6 never emails vendors directly; correspondence continues through the
Milestone 5 controlled communication workflow.
